# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to maintaining a respectful and constructive environment
for everyone contributing to Fluxora Modulith.

Expected behavior includes:

- Being respectful and professional.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Respecting different technical opinions.
- Focusing discussions on the project and its goals.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment.
- Personal attacks.
- Discriminatory comments.
- Threats.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject pull
requests, or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a healthy
project environment.
